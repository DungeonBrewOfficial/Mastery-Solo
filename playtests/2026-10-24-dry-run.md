# Dry Run, 2026-10-24

*A walk through the procedures with real dice (`audio/dice.py` from the mastery repo), by Claude, before Jon's first playtest. Fights were not played out: where a fight happened, its outcome was assumed so the procedures around it could be tested. The point was to find where the rules jam, not to test balance.*

**Party:** four level 1 characters: a Fighter, a Thief (Scout), Corra the Druid, and Gisla the Cursed. Autumn.

---

## Log

**The home.** 2d6 = 5: home is a village in Plains, with 1d3 + 1 = 2 roads, run east and west.

**The known lands** (terrain 2d6, then the neighbor d6 where needed; features 2d6):

| | | |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Forest (12; d6 2) | Hills (9; d6 3) | Forest (10; d6 2) |
| Hills (9; d6 3); the west road bends right (d6 4) | **Home**, Plains | Plains (8); the east road forks (d6 5) |
| Plains (8); features 11, nothing (no road) | Plains (8) | Plains (6); a river rises (9) |

**The rumor.** Gisla tries for rumors at the alehouse: Charisma d3 + Presence d2 = 3 against 4. Nothing.

**Day 1.** Weather (autumn d20) = 18: Hot, about 80°F. The party heads north at Normal pace.
- Leg 1: travel die 4, so 8 movement: 3 plains squares to the tile edge, then 2 hills squares (4). Event die 2: Quiet.
- Leg 2: travel die 5, but only 4 hours are left. They stop when the day runs out, moving 4 more hills squares to the northern edge of the hills tile. No event.
- Camp: night die 5. They ate iron rations, not a cooked meal, so nothing comes.

**Day 2.**
- Leg 1: travel die 2, so 4 movement. The party enters a blank tile: 2d6 = 10, the Hills row on a d6 of 5: **Mountains**. Features 6, nothing. One mountain square costs 3.
- Event die 3: **Sign.** Mountains, Band I, 2d6 = 11: a Wolf Beta's pack, above band. The Thief reads the signs: Perception d4 + Alertness d3 = 4 against the Even DC of 4. Success: about nine wolves. The party doesn't follow them.
- Leg 2: travel die 5, 10 movement: 3 more mountain squares. Event die 1: Quiet. One hour is left; the next travel die is sure to run past it.

**Day 3.** Weather 8: Cool. The party turns for home.
- Leg 1: travel die 1, so 2 movement. Not enough for a mountain square (3). They don't move. Event die 3: **Sign**, Mountains Band I 2d6 = 2: a Skeleton Warrior's cairn.
- Leg 2: travel die 5, 10 movement: 3 mountain squares. Event die 6: **Encounter.** Mountains Band I, 2d6 = 8: a Bandit Leader with two Cutthroats and a Cluster of Thugs [Raider], 6 XP.
  - Contact: the Thief's Perception, 2d4 = 4 against Division + 1 = 4: noticed. The Bandit Leader, d4 + d3 = 6 against 4: noticed. **Far**, both aware.
  - Disposition, Raider d6 = 2: **Toll.** They want payment. *How much? The rules don't say.*
  - The party refuses and fights. (Assumed: the party wins.) 6 XP each.
  - Purses: Leader 3d6 = 10, Cutthroats 2d6 = 10 and 5, the Cluster 1d6 = 2: 27 sp.
  - Salvage: the Cutthroats' scavenged jack of plate (listed Durability 38, worn to 14 before the fight, likely broken now). *Is a jack of plate metal armor for scrap?*
- Leg 3: travel die 3, past the end of the day. Camp. Night die 4.

**Day 4.** A Discovery, d12 = 11: **a lost place**, in the mountain tile. Lost places need Wild or Deep country. This tile has no road, and of its eight neighbors three are drawn (all without roads) and five are blank. *Do blank tiles count?* If they count as roadless, the tile is **Deep**, two tiles from home.
- In Deep country the guardian comes from the next band up: Lost place, Band II, d6 = 4: a Revenant Soldier with a Cluster of Skeletons (20 XP). The hoard is 40 × 20 = 800 sp, doubled for Deep: **1,600 sp**. The site has 2d6 + 2 = 6 areas, plus 2 for Deep.
- First area: contents d12 = 7, a lock or seal; passages d6 = 4, one way on.
- Exploration XP waiting at home, if they survive: Lost Location 75, plus every area, plus the hoard (20).

*Error in the log:* on Days 2 and 3, the mountain tile was Deep (if blank tiles count as roadless), so the encounter tables should have been rolled one band up. That's a sign of how easy the rule is to forget.

## What jammed

**Gaps filled with a default** (marked [N] in the design files, and listed here so Jon can overrule them):

1. **Reading signs had no DC.** Now the band's Even DC, the same as following a fresh trail (`01-the-turn.md`).
2. **Raiders' toll had no amount.** Now the head's XP value × 5 sp per character: the Bandit Leader (6 XP) asks 30 sp each (`02-encounters.md`).
3. **Blank tiles and Deep country.** Now decided when a tile is generated: blank neighbors count as having no road. The frontier starts out Deep, and a tile already drawn doesn't change when a road turns up next to it later (`04-the-map.md`).
4. **A wolf den with 400 sp in it.** A beast's hoard is what its victims carried: purses, a dead traveler's pack, a cart's goods, scattered among the bones (`05-sites.md`).

**Questions for Jon** (answered 2026-10-24: 5 and 6 yes; 7 daily trials; 9 fine as is; 10 hidden and lost places reseeded through clues and lost lore; 11 scrap for any armor; 8 stays as is, since going too deep too early should be deadly):

5. **The day's last leg has no event.** When the travel die runs past the end of the day, the party stops without an event, so the last few hours of every day are safe. Over a day that's roughly one leg in three. Should a cut-short leg still roll the event die?
6. **Leftover movement is lost.** A 1-hour leg at Normal pace gives 2 movement, which can't buy a mountain or swamp square (3), so the party doesn't move at all. Should leftover movement carry over to the next leg within the same day?
7. **Weather trials are heavy on paper.** Hot weather asks for an Endurance trial every 2 hours per character: 16 trials a day for four characters [M]. One trial per character per day, at the worst conditions of the day, would carry the same risk with far less rolling.
8. **Deep country comes early.** Home had two roads, both running east and west, so a tile two north of home was already Deep. For a Band I party in Deep country, most encounters become Signs (the next band up is mostly above band). Is that the feel you want, or should the known lands around home always be at least Wild?
9. **Deep sites stack up.** A Deep site doubles the hoard and draws its guardian from the next band up, and the guardian's XP sets the hoard. Here a level 1 party found a 1,600 sp hoard behind a Tier 2 Champion: a jackpot worth almost half a suit of plate. The guardian is above band, so the party is warned and can walk away, but if they beat it, they jump ahead. Intended greed, or too much?
10. **Exploration XP at low level.** A lost place pays 75 XP as a location, plus its areas and the hoard: about 115 XP, more than enough to reach level 2 (100) from one site, against 4 to 6 XP a fight. That's Mastery's table as written. It makes sites the engine of early levels, which may be exactly right.
11. **What counts as metal armor for scrap?** Jack of plate, jazerant, and brigandine are metal sewn into cloth or leather.

**Worked as hoped:**

- The tile generator made a readable map in a few minutes, and mountains came out of hills the way they should.
- Contact and disposition went quickly: two rolls and a d6, and the result (a toll demanded at Far) gave the party a real choice before any fight.
- Signs did their job. The party learned about a wolf pack it had no business fighting and chose not to follow.
- The mountain detour cost a full day, and it felt like it: terrain cost is doing what it should.
